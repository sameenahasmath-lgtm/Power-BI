# Campaign Insights Summary

## Overview
This analysis reviews eight weeks of campaign performance to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities for optimization.

## Key Observations
CTR increases steadily from Week 1 to Week 4, then begins to decline from Week 5 onward. This decline happens despite impressions increasing significantly in Weeks 6 and 7, indicating lower-quality traffic or creative fatigue.

Conversions peak around Week 4–5 and then drop sharply in Weeks 6–7, suggesting that the additional impressions are not translating into meaningful engagement.

## Metric Patterns
- **Impressions:** Strong upward trend, especially Weeks 6–7
- **Clicks:** Do not increase proportionally with impressions
- **CTR:** Declines from Week 5 onward
- **Conversions:** Highest in Week 4–5, lowest in Week 7
- **Spend:** Increases but does not improve performance

## Possible Reasons
- Creative fatigue after Week 5
- Audience expansion leading to lower-quality traffic
- Placement mix shifting toward lower-performing inventory
- Misalignment between creative messaging and new audience segments

## Recommendations
- Refresh creatives with new variations and stronger CTAs
- Reduce spend on low-performing audience segments
- Shift delivery back to high-performing placements
- Run A/B tests to validate creative and audience performance
- Monitor CTR recovery over the next 7–10 days
