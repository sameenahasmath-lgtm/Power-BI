# Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
## Issue: CTR Dropped by 40% Week-over-Week

### 📌 Summary
During Week 12, the Click-Through Rate (CTR) dropped from **2.5% → 1.5%**, a **40% decline**.  
This RCA identifies the most likely causes, supporting data signals, and recommended actions.

---

## 1️⃣ What Changed? (Initial Observations)
- Impressions increased by **+32%**
- Clicks decreased by **–18%**
- CPC increased slightly (**+6%**)
- Two new creatives were launched
- Budget was shifted toward a new audience segment

These signals suggest **traffic quality changed**, not platform issues.

---

## 2️⃣ Possible Root Causes

### **A. Audience Quality Declined**
- New audience segment had **lower engagement history**
- Higher impressions but fewer clicks
- CTR dropped mainly in the new segment

**Evidence:**  
CTR in old audience = **2.7%**  
CTR in new audience = **0.9%**

---

### **B. Creative Fatigue**
- Main creative has been running for **5+ weeks**
- Drop in unique CTR indicates users have seen it too many times

**Evidence:**  
Frequency increased from **2.1 → 3.4**

---

### **C. Poor Creative-Audience Match**
- New creatives had different messaging style  
- Misalignment with audience intent
